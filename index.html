The problem

Students are rich in skills and broke in cash.

Private tutoring costs ₹500–₹1,500 an hour. Campus talent is everywhere, but there's no trusted way to trade it. So students pay money they don't have — or learn nothing at all.

Tutoring is unaffordable

Coaching centres and private tutors price out the exact students who need help the most. A single subject can cost a month's pocket money.

Informal skills go unused

A guitarist, a coder, a fluent French speaker — every hostel floor is full of teachers. There's just no marketplace to turn that knowledge into value.

Strangers can't be trusted

Meeting a random peer to "swap skills" has no protection. Without escrow and verified profiles, both sides risk ghosting, no-shows and scams.

The barter engine

One hour taught. One credit earned. One hour learned.

Time is the only currency. Every swap is locked in escrow and released only when both people confirm the session happened.

Core features

Built for trust, speed and zero cash.

Every feature exists to remove one barrier: cost, trust, matching, or time.

Time-Credit Escrow Vault

Credits lock before the session and auto-release after dual confirmation. Ghosting, no-shows and scams lose their payday.

AI Skill Matching

Tell the vault what you want to learn. Matching ranks peers by skill fit, availability, rating and past swap success — not by who paid for ads.

Verified Trust Badges

ID and portfolio verification for serious swappers. A badge signals "this person shows up" before the first message is even sent.

Hybrid Cash Bridge

Need to learn today? Buy an affordable starter credit pack over UPI. Cash buys time credits — never people's labour directly.

Zero-Cash Swaps

No commission on peer swaps. Earn and spend in the same currency: hours. The platform monetises convenience, not every transaction.

Campus Communities

Every college gets its own vault. Swap inside your hostel, department or club — with people you can actually meet in the library.

Monetization

Free to swap. Paid to go faster.

The core barter loop stays free forever. Revenue comes from power users, instant-credit buyers and credibility seekers.

Campus rollout strategy

One campus at a time. Depth before reach.

A dense campus beats a scattered city: swaps are in-person-friendly, trust spreads by word of mouth, and one success story recruits a whole hostel.

Week 1 · Seed Recruit 30 founding swappers from one department. Give each 5 credits and hand-verify profiles for free.
Week 2–3 · First 100 swaps Host a "Teach Tuesday" swap fair in the library. Target 100 completed, escrow-confirmed sessions.
Month 2 · Campus ambassadors Convert top swappers into ambassadors with Pro accounts. Launch referral credits and club partnerships.
Month 3 · Expand Copy the playbook to three neighbouring campuses. Showcase retention, swap volume and Pro conversion.
