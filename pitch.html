Run sheet

Who says what.

Each speaker owns one idea. No overlap, no repeated lines. Handover happens on the final sentence, not after it.

01

The Hook & Problem

Speaker 1 · opens the pitch
0:00 – 2:00
  • Open with a question: "How many of you have paid for a skill you could have learned from the person sitting next to you?"
  • Name the pain: private tutoring costs ₹500–₹1,500 an hour. Students are rich in skills and broke in cash.
  • Show the waste: every campus has coders, guitarists and French speakers whose skills never get traded.
  • State the third problem: no trust layer. Meeting a stranger to swap skills has no protection.
Handover line: "So we asked a simple question — what if the currency was time, not money?"
02

The Solution & Live UI Mockup

Speaker 2 · runs the product demo
2:00 – 4:00
  • Introduce SkillVault: peer-to-peer skill barter on campus, with 1 hour taught = 1 Time Credit.
  • Signal up on the projector, claim the 5 starter credits live — the number visibly appears in the balance pill.
  • Demonstrate the loop: search a skill, book a session, show the credit locking in escrow.
  • Confirm the session and let escrow release on screen. Point at the ledger entry as proof it settled.
Demo rule: narrate the "why", not the clicks. Say "notice the credit is locked, not paid" — that line does the work.
03

Core Features & Trust Engine

Speaker 3 · the engine room
4:00 – 6:00
  • Time-Credit Escrow Vault: credits lock at booking and release only after dual confirmation. Ghosting earns nothing.
  • AI Skill Matching: ranks peers by skill fit, availability, rating and swap history — not by who paid for ads.
  • Verified Trust Badges: ID and portfolio verification for top swappers, so credibility arrives before the first message.
  • Hybrid Cash Bridge: ₹299 starter packs on UPI for people who want to learn before they earn.
Handover line: "That trust engine is free for students. So where does the money come from?"
04

Business Model & Revenue

Speaker 4 · the numbers
6:00 – 8:00
  • Core barter stays free forever — that protects adoption and word of mouth.
  • SkillVault Pro at ₹199/month: unlimited swaps plus priority ranking for power users.
  • Credit Top-Up Packs at ₹299 for instant credits, and Verified Badges at ₹499 one-time for credibility.
  • Anchor it with the 5,000-student scenario: an illustrative ₹3.09 lakh monthly revenue at conservative conversion.
Frame it honestly: "We monetise convenience and credibility, never the barter itself."
05

Social Proof, Rollout & Close

Speaker 5 · closes and calls to action
8:00 – 10:00
  • Social proof: Priya traded three Python hours for guitar lessons; Arjun says escrow is the reason he shows up.
  • Rollout: one campus at a time. 30 founding swappers, 100 confirmed swaps, then ambassadors and three neighbouring campuses.
  • Why we win: dense campus networks make trust spread faster than any ad budget can buy.
  • Close with the ask: pilot SkillVault in our own college this semester and be our first 50 users.
Closing line: "You already have something worth teaching. SkillVault turns it into something worth learning."

Why free stays free

Charging commission on swaps would kill the zero-cash promise. Revenue comes from speed (top-ups), priority (Pro) and trust (badges).

Cost structure

Hosting and matching are low-cost at campus scale. Biggest spend is campus ambassadors, paid in Pro accounts and referral credits.

Honest limitation

These are projections on 5,000 users, not actuals. Validation milestone: 100 completed swaps with 30% week-4 retention before spending on growth.

Q&A prep

The six questions teachers will ask.

Answers are short on purpose. One sentence of substance, one sentence of evidence.

"What stops someone from faking a session?"Escrow needs dual confirmation, and repeat fraud gets the badge revoked. Credits only move after both sides agree the hour happened.
"Why not just use cash or UPI?"Cash prices out the students who need help most. Time equalises value: an hour of guitar is worth an hour of Python.
"How do you get the first 100 users?"Hand-recruit 30 founding swappers from one department, then run a "Teach Tuesday" swap fair in the library.
"Is this a real payment product?"This prototype simulates credits locally in the browser. Production would need a licensed payment partner for top-ups.
"What about safety and harassment?"Verified profiles, campus-only matching, ratings after every swap, and a report flow that freezes escrow during review.
"Why would anyone pay for Pro?"Heavy swappers want priority ranking and unlimited swaps. At ₹199 it costs less than one hour of private tutoring.
